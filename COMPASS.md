# Compass

mateoroldos.com is Mateo Roldos’s home for learning, building, and publishing.

> How do we build excellent software when building becomes almost free?

AI makes production abundant. Judgment, taste, and understanding matter more.
The goal is better work, not more output.

- Study durable ideas, inside and outside software. Understand them in context.
- Notice what others miss. Test ideas against real problems.
- Edit ruthlessly. Simplicity is the result of selection and restraint.
- Share useful artifacts and honest observations. Let feedback deepen the work.
- Capture questions before starting projects. Let real needs justify new tools.

The site should feel like a workshop and independent publication: calm, curious,
and human. Never manufacture anxiety for attention. Leave room for personality.

Keep publishing easy, formats flexible, and maintenance low. This site is the
canonical home; GitHub and communities help the work travel and invite conversation.
Publish when something deserves attention, without a required cadence.
